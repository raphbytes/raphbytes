## About Me

I'm a developer with a depth-first approach to learning — I'd rather deeply understand how one thing works than skim the surface of ten. Right now that means working from software down toward hardware: not just using tools, but understanding *why* they work the way they do. I build in the way that makes sense to me, on my own terms — not to keep up with anyone else's timeline. I enjoy working with people — collaboration shows me perspectives and problem-solving approaches I wouldn't land on alone.

## 🚧 Currently Building

**[Nexera](#)** — a cinematic data visualization portfolio SPA built with vanilla JavaScript, D3.js, and Three.js.

It's both a serious technical vehicle for learning and a portfolio piece: a two-zone architecture (lightweight marketing/auth shell + a heavy dashboard SPA), an Express backend on Render, MongoDB Atlas, deployed via Vercel/Netlify. Seven dashboards, eight visualizations — from a music industry revenue streamgraph to a Sankey energy flow diagram.
